About Me

Priyadharshan

Python Backend Developer

I'm Priyadharshan, a Python Developer focused on backend development, platform integrations, and helpdesk data migration. My professional experience began with DataGrine, where I've been developing a migration platform that helps businesses move their support data between systems.

At DataGrine, I've built migrations for Freshdesk, Intercom, Zendesk, and Salesforce. Working across these platforms has given me practical experience with the complexity of connecting different systems and moving business data between them. I'm currently extending this work to Outlook and Gmail migrations.

As my first professional project, DataGrine has shaped how I approach software development: understand the requirements, work through integration challenges, and build solutions around the people who depend on them. It has strengthened my interest in backend engineering and the work involved in making separate platforms function together.

My technical foundation includes Python, Django, Flask, REST APIs, MySQL, and MongoDB. Alongside my development work, I have hands-on experience with AWS, Docker, and Jenkins, supported by training in Terraform, CloudFormation, and Kubernetes. This gives me a broader understanding of application development, deployment, and infrastructure.

I'm continuing to grow as a developer through practical engineering challenges, with a focus on building dependable backend applications and integrations. I'm interested in opportunities where I can contribute this experience, take on greater responsibility, and deepen my technical skills.
